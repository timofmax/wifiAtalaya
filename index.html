Privacy Policy for WiFi Atalaya

Effective date: September 30, 2026

WiFi Atalaya ("the app") is a Mac and iPhone app for managing Wi-Fi networks on Juniper Mist. This policy explains what the app does with your data. The developer runs no servers of its own and does not sell your data.

Information the app works with

The app uses a Juniper Mist API token that you provide. With it, the app reads data from the Mist API, such as organization and site details, service-level metrics, access point status, Wi-Fi network settings, and client records like MAC addresses, IP addresses, usernames and device types. If your token allows it, the app also changes settings in your Mist organization when you ask it to, for example when you add, rename or restart an access point.

Where your data goes

The app talks directly to the Juniper Mist API. Your API token and the network data it reads are not sent to the developer.

How your API token is stored

The token is saved in a file in the app's own storage on your Mac or iPhone. It is only sent to the Juniper Mist API, to authenticate your requests.

Local caching

To stay within Mist's API rate limits, the app may keep recent responses on your device. They are not sent anywhere.

Analytics

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to see which screens and actions are used. An event records the screen or action, the organization and site names and IDs, the access level of your token, and the access point model. Events never contain your API token, MAC addresses, IP addresses or other client data. The app also reads its feature settings from Google Firebase using an anonymous sign-in. Apple may share crash and usage data with the developer if you opted in to sharing analytics with app developers in your device settings.

Third-party services

Your use of Juniper Mist is governed by Juniper Networks' privacy practices. Analytics data is handled by Google under the Firebase terms and Google's privacy policy.

Data retention and deletion

You can remove the stored token and cached data by signing out in the app or by deleting the app. Analytics data is kept by Google Firebase for its configured retention period.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for IT and networking professionals and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy
